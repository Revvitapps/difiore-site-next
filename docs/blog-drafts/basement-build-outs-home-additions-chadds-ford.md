## **🛠️ What You Can (Maybe) Do Yourself**

Basements and additions aren’t your average DIY project — but there are a few prep and finishing tasks homeowners can realistically tackle.

### **👍 DIY-Friendly Tasks (With Caution):**

* **Planning and vision boarding** – Create mood boards, take measurements, and sketch layout ideas

* **Painting finished walls** – Once the pros are done, this can save a bit on labor

* **Installing flooring** – Vinyl planks or carpet tiles in dry, finished basements

* **Decorating and furnishing** – Lighting, shelving, and design touches

That said, the *actual construction and permitting process?* That’s best left to the pros — unless you want to play a real-life game of “Code Violation Roulette.”
