## **💡 Smart Remodeling Tips from the Pros**

Want to make the most of your remodel? Here are a few gems from the DiFiore Builders crew:

* **Plan for workflow** – Think *fridge, stove, sink* — the classic kitchen triangle still works

* **Don’t skimp on lighting** – Layered lighting (ambient, task, accent) makes a massive difference

* **Add outlets where you’ll need them** – Think about where you’ll use mixers, coffee machines, and charging stations

* **Use drawers over lower cabinets** – Easier access, better organization

* **Think long-term** – Trends are great, but choose core finishes that will still look good in 10 years

## **🧰 DIY Tip: Start Small While You Plan Big**

Not ready for a full-blown remodel yet? Try these light DIY refreshes to make your current kitchen more livable:

* Swap out cabinet hardware for an instant style boost

* Add LED strip lighting under cabinets for mood \+ function

* Use open shelving to reduce visual clutter

* Paint an accent wall or island base to add a pop of color

* Organize your pantry with bins and labels — functional *and* pretty\!

These small wins can help you get clarity on what you *really* want when it’s time to go big.
