## **🧰 Seasonal Roofing Maintenance Tips**

Want to make your roof last longer? Set up a basic maintenance routine. You don’t need to climb up there with a hammer — just a visual inspection and a few small actions can make a big difference.

### **🔁 Spring & Fall Maintenance Checklist:**

* **Clear gutters and downspouts** – Clogged gutters \= water backing onto your roof

* **Look for missing, cracked, or curling shingles**

* **Trim overhanging branches** – Prevents debris and animal damage

* **Check attic for leaks after storms** – Especially around chimneys and vents

* **Inspect flashing and seals** – Pay attention to skylights, pipes, and vents

💡 **Pro Tip:** After a major storm or heavy snowfall, do a quick visual check from the ground or attic. DiFiore Builders also offers professional inspections if you’d rather stay safely on the ground.

## **🕵️ How to Tell If You Need a New Roof**

It’s the million-dollar question (or hopefully more like $10–15K): *Do I need a full roof replacement, or just a repair?*

Here are some clear signs your roof may be ready to retire:

### **🚩 Warning Signs:**

* **Shingles are missing, loose, or look like they’re shedding granules**

* **Your roof is 20–25 years old or more**

* **Interior water damage or ceiling stains**

* **Moss, algae, or dark streaks** – A sign of moisture retention

* **Daylight visible through roof boards in the attic**

* **High energy bills** – Poor insulation or ventilation can mean roofing problems

If you’re seeing multiple red flags, it’s best to get a professional roof inspection ASAP.
